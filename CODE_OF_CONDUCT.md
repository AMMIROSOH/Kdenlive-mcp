# Code of conduct

Contributors and maintainers must keep project spaces respectful, inclusive, and
focused on constructive technical collaboration. Harassment, threats, personal
attacks, discriminatory language, doxxing, and publication of private media or
credentials are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers may
edit or remove content, restrict participation, or ban accounts when necessary to
protect participants and the project. Good-faith technical disagreement is welcome;
focus criticism on code, evidence, risk, and tradeoffs rather than people.
